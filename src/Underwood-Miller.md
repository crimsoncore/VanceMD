# Underwood-Miller: Publications in Chronological Order
Underwood-Miller was a San Francisco/Columbia, PA specialty small press founded in 1976 by Tim Underwood and Chuck Miller. Its first book was a first hardcover edition of *The Dying Earth* — Vance became "the author most identified with the press," and over the next eighteen years Underwood-Miller produced the first hardcover (and in several cases first-ever book) editions of a large share of Vance's back catalog, plus several reference works about him. The partnership dissolved in 1994; its last book was a second reprint of *The Dying Earth*, closing the circle back to its first.
## 1976
The press's debut title.
- *The Dying Earth* — 1st hardcover ed. of the 1950 story-cycle
## 1977
- *Fantasy and Science Fiction by Jack Vance* (Tim Underwood & Chuck Miller) — bibliography chapbook
## 1978
- *The Eyes of the Overworld* — 1st hardcover ed. (Cugel the Clever/Dying Earth #2)
- *Big Planet* — 1st hardcover ed.
- *Fantasms: A Bibliography of the Literature of Jack Vance* (Daniel J. H. Levack & Tim Underwood) — bibliography; placed 5th, Best Reference Book, 1979 Locus Awards
- *The View from Chickweed's Window* — 1st book ed. of this mystery, as "John Holbrook Vance"
## 1979
Underwood-Miller's busiest year, spanning Planet of Adventure, Dying Earth chapbooks, and two mystery firsts.
- *The House on Lily Street* — 1st book ed. of this mystery, as "John Holbrook Vance"
- *City of the Chasch* — 1st hardcover ed. (Planet of Adventure #1)
- *The Seventeen Virgins* — chapbook (Cugel/Dying Earth novella)
- *The Bagful of Dreams* — chapbook (Cugel/Dying Earth novella)
- *The Languages of Pao* — 1st hardcover ed.
- *The Blue World* — 1st hardcover ed.
- *Green Magic: The Fantasy Realms of Jack Vance* — collection
- *Morreion: A Tale of the Dying Earth* — chapbook (Rhialto/Dying Earth novella, later revised for the 1984 *Rhialto the Marvellous*)
- *Fantasms II: A Bibliography of the Works of Jack Vance* (Levack, Underwood & Kurt Cockrum) — revised bibliography, published by K. Cockrum rather than under the Underwood-Miller imprint itself
## 1980
- *Servants of the Wankh* — 1st hardcover ed. (Planet of Adventure #2)
- *The Dirdir* — 1st hardcover ed. (Planet of Adventure #3)
- *The Face* — 1st hardcover ed. (Demon Princes #4)
- *Galactic Effectuator* — collection (the Miro Hetzel novellas)
- *The Last Castle* — standalone illustrated chapbook of the 1966 Hugo/Nebula novella
- *Jack Vance* (ed. Underwood & Miller) — critical essay anthology, Taplinger's "Writers of the 21st Century" series; published by Taplinger, not under the Underwood-Miller imprint
## 1981
- *The Pnume* — 1st hardcover ed. (Planet of Adventure #4)
- *The Book of Dreams* — 1st hardcover ed. (Demon Princes #5)
- *The Star King* — hardcover reissue (Demon Princes #1; first pub. Galaxy 1963–64/Berkley 1964)
- *The Killing Machine* — hardcover reissue (Demon Princes #2)
- *The Palace of Love* — hardcover reissue (Demon Princes #3)
## 1982
- *Gold and Iron* — hardcover reissue under Vance's preferred title (= *Slaves of the Klau*/*Planet of the Damned*, first pub. 1952/1958)
- *Lost Moons* — collection gathering several previously uncollected early stories
- *Bad Ronald* — hardcover ed. of this mystery
## 1983
A year dominated by reissues — the Durdane trilogy in a matching slipcased set, plus several chapbooks and mysteries.
- *The Houses of Iszm* — chapbook, 700 copies
- *Son of the Tree* — chapbook
- *The Magnificent Showboats of the Lower Vissel River, Lune XXIII South, Big Planet* — 1st hardcover ed. under this author-preferred title (= *Showboat World*, Pyramid 1975; Big Planet #2)
- *The Man in the Cage* — hardcover reissue of the Edgar-winning mystery (first pub. Random House 1960)
- *The Faceless Man* — hardcover reissue (= *The Anome*, Durdane #1), slipcased with the two below
- *The Brave Free Men* — hardcover reissue (Durdane #2)
- *The Asutra* — hardcover reissue (Durdane #3)
- *Suldrun's Garden: Lyonesse I* — hardcover ed.; most bibliographies credit Berkeley Books' 1983 mass-market paperback as the true first book edition, so this U-M hardcover's exact status (parallel edition vs. a listing error) is unresolved
## 1984
- *Space Opera* — hardcover reissue (first pub. Pyramid 1965)
- *Cugel's Saga* — 1st hardcover ed. (Dying Earth #3; the first-ever book publication was Timescape/Pocket Books' 1983 mass-market paperback). Sources disagree on the U-M edition's exact date, giving either 1983 or May 1984.
- *Trullion: Alastor 2262* — hardcover reissue (Alastor #1)
- *Marune: Alastor 933* — hardcover reissue (Alastor #2)
- *Wyst: Alastor 1716* — hardcover reissue (Alastor #3)
- *Rhialto the Marvellous* — Dying Earth #4; published under the **Brandywyne Books** imprint (Underwood-Miller's separate, all-genre limited-editions line launched with Waldenbooks in the mid-1980s), not "Underwood-Miller" proper
## 1985
- *The Complete Magnus Ridolph* — collection of the Magnus Ridolph stories; sources disagree on the exact date, giving either 1984 or January 1985
- *The Green Pearl: Lyonesse II* — book-original 1st ed.
- *Strange Notions* — 1st book ed. (= *Strange People, Queer Notions*, Vance's most-delayed mystery to reach print)
- *The Dark Ocean* — 1st book ed. of this mystery, issued in a shared slipcase with *Strange Notions*
## 1986
- *The Augmented Agent and Other Stories* — collection
- *The Dark Side of the Moon* — collection, 14 stories plus an author's introduction
## 1987
- *Araminta Station* — book-original 1st ed. (Cadwal Chronicles #1)
## 1988
- *Bird Isle* — 1st book ed. under this author-preferred title (= *Isle of Peril*), usually sold as a 2-volume signed/boxed set with the title below
- *Take My Face* — hardcover ed. (= *The Flesh Mask*)
## 1989
- *Madouc: Lyonesse III* — book-original 1st ed.; World Fantasy Award, Best Novel, 1990
## 1990
- *Chateau d'If and Other Stories* — collection
## 1991
- *Ecce and Old Earth* — book-original 1st ed. (Cadwal Chronicles #2)
## 1992
- *Throy* — book-original 1st ed. (Cadwal Chronicles #3)
- *When the Five Moons Rise* — collection
- *The Jack Vance Lexicon: From Ahulph to Zipangote* (ed. Dan Temianka) — a glossary of Vance's invented/coined vocabulary
## 1993
- *The Five Gold Bands* — hardcover reissue (= *The Space Pirate*/*The Rapparee*, first pub. 1950/1953)
## 1994
The partnership's final year.
- *The Dying Earth* — hardcover reprint, the press's last book, closing the circle back to its 1976 debut
- *The Work of Jack Vance* (Jerry Hewett & Daryl F. Mallett, ed. Boden Clarke) — bibliography that became the standard Vance reference for years afterward; cited elsewhere in this project's `VIE.md`
## Notes
- Several interviews and essays by Tim Underwood appear inside other publications rather than as standalone books, among them "A Talk with Jack Vance" (1985), the Dutch-language "Orbit Interview: Jack Vance" (1981), the afterword "Jack Vance: The Man and the Myth" (1994), and the three-part retrospective "Ten Years of Underwood-Miller" (1986, with Chuck Miller).
- Sources consulted: ISFDB (publisher, title, and publication records); vancemuseum.com's *Jack Vance Underwood-Miller Bibliography* (covers editions "up through 1992" with month-level dates and binding/limitation detail, but has not been rechecked against a live source); the Wikipedia "Underwood–Miller" article's publication list (comprehensive but year-level only); pagesofpages.com's Vance checklist (collection contents, not independently re-verified against ISFDB for every title); and this project's own `VIE.md` for cross-checking true first-publication dates.
- Open items: the *View from Chickweed's Window* 1978-vs-1979 date conflict with `VIE.md`; the *Cugel's Saga* and *Complete Magnus Ridolph* date discrepancies above; whether *Suldrun's Garden: Lyonesse I* was really a distinct parallel hardcover; and independent re-verification of the *Green Magic*, *Lost Moons*, *The Complete Magnus Ridolph*, *When the Five Moons Rise*, and *Chateau d'If* contents directly against ISFDB.
