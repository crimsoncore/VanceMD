# Underwood-Miller: Publications in Chronological Order
Underwood-Miller was a San Francisco/Columbia, PA specialty small press founded in 1976 by Tim Underwood and Chuck Miller. Its first book was a first hardcover edition of *The Dying Earth* — Vance became "the author most identified with the press," and over the next eighteen years Underwood-Miller produced the first hardcover (and in several cases first-ever book) editions of a large share of Vance's back catalog, plus several reference works about him. The partnership dissolved in 1994; its last book was a second reprint of *The Dying Earth*, closing the circle back to its first. Tim Underwood then founded a separate solo imprint, **Underwood Books** (Grass Valley/Nevada City, CA) — primarily an art-book publisher, but responsible for one significant later Vance title; see that section below. Chuck Miller does not appear to have continued publishing Vance titles solo after 1994.
## 1976
The press's debut title.
- *The Dying Earth* — 1st hardcover ed. of the 1950 story-cycle
## 1977
- *Fantasy and Science Fiction by Jack Vance* (Tim Underwood & Chuck Miller) — bibliography chapbook
## 1978
- *The Eyes of the Overworld* — 1st hardcover ed. (Cugel the Clever/Dying Earth #2)
- *Big Planet* — 1st hardcover ed.
- *Fantasms: A Bibliography of the Literature of Jack Vance* (Daniel J. H. Levack & Tim Underwood) — bibliography; placed 5th, Best Reference Book, 1979 Locus Awards
- *The View from Chickweed's Window* — 1st book ed. of this mystery, as "John Holbrook Vance"

- *The Bagful of Dreams* — chapbook (Cugel/Dying Earth novella)

## 1983
A year dominated by reissues — the Durdane trilogy in a matching slipcased set, plus several chapbooks and mysteries.
- *The Houses of Iszm* — chapbook, 700 copies
- *Son of the Tree* — chapbook
- *The Magnificent Showboats of the Lower Vissel River, Lune XXIII South, Big Planet* — 1st hardcover ed. under this author-preferred title (= *Showboat World*, Pyramid 1975; Big Planet #2)

- *The Faceless Man* — hardcover reissue (= *The Anome*, Durdane #1), slipcased with the two below
- *The Brave Free Men* — hardcover reissue (Durdane #2)
- *The Asutra* — hardcover reissue (Durdane #3)
- *Suldrun's Garden: Lyonesse I* — hardcover ed.; most bibliographies credit Berkeley Books' 1983 mass-market paperback as the true first book edition, so this U-M hardcover's exact status (parallel edition vs. a listing error) is unresolved
## 1984
- *Space Opera* — hardcover reissue (first pub. Pyramid 1965)
- *Cugel's Saga* — 1st hardcover ed. (Dying Earth #3; the first-ever book publication was Timescape/Pocket Books' 1983 mass-market paperback). Sources disagree on the U-M edition's exact date, giving either 1983 or May 1984.

- *Marune: Alastor 933* — hardcover reissue (Alastor #2)
- *Wyst: Alastor 1716* — hardcover reissue (Alastor #3)
- *Rhialto the Marvellous* — Dying Earth #4; published under the **Brandywyne Books** imprint (Underwood-Miller's separate, all-genre limited-editions line launched with Waldenbooks in the mid-1980s), not "Underwood-Miller" proper
## 1985

- *Bird Isle* — 1st book ed. under this author-preferred title (= *Isle of Peril*), usually sold as a 2-volume signed/boxed set with the title below

## 1989

## 1990
- *Chateau d'If and Other Stories* — collection

## 1993
- *The Five Gold Bands* — hardcover reissue (= *The Space Pirate*/*The Rapparee*, first pub. 1950/1953)
## 1994
The partnership's final year.
- *The Dying Earth* — hardcover reprint, the press's last book, closing the circle back to its 1976 debut
- *The Work of Jack Vance* (Jerry Hewett & Daryl F. Mallett, ed. Boden Clarke) — bibliography that became the standard Vance reference for years afterward; cited elsewhere in this project's `VIE.md`
## Underwood Books (Tim Underwood, solo, post-1994)
After Underwood-Miller closed, Tim Underwood founded **Underwood Books** (Grass Valley/Nevada City, CA) in 1994, specializing mostly in fantasy/SF art books (the long-running *Spectrum* annual anthology, Frank Frazetta and Jeffrey Jones art books, etc.) and reissued Philip K. Dick and Stephen King material. **It also quietly published the true first editions of Vance's last two novels** — a fact easy to miss because the more widely-seen Tor trade hardcovers followed within months and are what most readers and even some checklists cite as "the" first edition:
- ***Night Lamp*** (1996; Gaean Reach) — **Underwood Books, April 1996**: limited/signed edition of 600 numbered copies, cover by Stephen Hickman, ISBN 1-887424-18-0, $60. Tor's trade hardcover followed in **November 1996** ($23.95). Confirmed by ISFDB, Wikipedia's own infobox for the novel, SFE picture gallery, and a dealer listing (L.W. Currey) for the Underwood limited edition.
- ***Ports of Call*** (1998; Ports of Call/Gaean Reach #1) — **Underwood Books, January 1998**: limited/signed edition, cover by Rick Berry, ISBN 1-887424-39-3, viii+239pp, $60. Tor's trade hardcover followed in **April 1998** ($24.95). Confirmed by ISFDB and a Dutch review site citing "Eerste druk: Underwood Books, Nevada City."
- ***Lurulu*** (2004; Ports of Call #2), by contrast, appears to have been a **Tor-only** first edition — no Underwood Books edition has been found for it, suggesting the practice of an Underwood limited edition preceding the Tor trade edition did not continue past *Ports of Call*.
- ***The Laughing Magician*** — an omnibus combining *The Eyes of the Overworld* and *Cugel's Saga* (Dying Earth #2–3), illustrated throughout by Stephen Fabian, reworking art from the 1977 Underwood-Miller *Eyes of the Overworld*. ISBN 1-887424-00-8 (978-1-887424-00-4).
- **Publication history is unusually tangled for a Vance title**, and sources disagree:
  - First announced as in-progress in a February 1995 fan newsletter (*The Vance Phile* #5), tentatively "ready in May" at $75, signed/numbered/slipcased.
  - Still being announced as forthcoming in late 2000–2002 fan-site news (release dates given as March 2001, then "early 2002"); at least one buyer's Amazon order was pushed back to a March 2001 release that itself apparently slipped.
  - pagesofpages.com's checklist dates the book's contents to 1997.
  - ISFDB's title record lists it as **"unpublished"** outright (with a full collation — 358pp, cover by Fabian — as if a full production existed but the book itself may not have shipped in that form).
  - Yet multiple retailer records (Amazon, eCampus, AuthorWars) show it as an actually-sold hardcover with a **2011 or 2012** publication date (Amazon/eCampus both give 27 Nov 2012; books-by-isbn.com gives 2011), suggesting it may finally have been released roughly a decade and a half after it was first announced — or that retailer metadata is simply wrong/recycled from a listing that was never fulfilled.
  - **Net assessment**: treat *The Laughing Magician*'s existence as a real, working title with confirmed cover art and an assigned ISBN, but its actual publication date and even whether copies were ever generally distributed are unresolved; do not cite a single date for it without flagging this discrepancy.
## Notes
- Several interviews and essays by Tim Underwood appear inside other publications rather than as standalone books, among them "A Talk with Jack Vance" (1985), the Dutch-language "Orbit Interview: Jack Vance" (1981), the afterword "Jack Vance: The Man and the Myth" (1994), and the three-part retrospective "Ten Years of Underwood-Miller" (1986, with Chuck Miller).
- Sources consulted: ISFDB (publisher, title, and publication records); vancemuseum.com's *Jack Vance Underwood-Miller Bibliography* (covers editions "up through 1992" with month-level dates and binding/limitation detail, but has not been rechecked against a live source); the Wikipedia "Underwood–Miller" article's publication list (comprehensive but year-level only); pagesofpages.com's Vance checklist (collection contents, not independently re-verified against ISFDB for every title); vancemuseum.com's *Jack Vance Information Archive* (dated fan-news items, used for *The Laughing Magician*'s publication timeline); books-by-isbn.com and retailer listings (Amazon, eCampus, AuthorWars) for Underwood Books ISBN-block data; and this project's own `VIE.md` for cross-checking true first-publication dates.
- Open items: the *View from Chickweed's Window* 1978-vs-1979 date conflict with `VIE.md`; the *Cugel's Saga* and *Complete Magnus Ridolph* date discrepancies above; whether *Suldrun's Garden: Lyonesse I* was really a distinct parallel hardcover; independent re-verification of the *Green Magic*, *Lost Moons*, *The Complete Magnus Ridolph*, *When the Five Moons Rise*, and *Chateau d'If* contents directly against ISFDB; and *The Laughing Magician*'s true publication date/status (see above).
