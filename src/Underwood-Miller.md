# Underwood-Miller: A Jack Vance Publication Chronology

Underwood-Miller was a San Francisco/Columbia, PA specialty small press founded in 1976 by Tim Underwood (a San Francisco book/art dealer) and Chuck Miller (a Pennsylvania used-book dealer). Its very first book was a first hardcover edition of *The Dying Earth* (1976) — Vance became "the author most identified with the press," and over the next eighteen years Underwood-Miller produced the first hardcover (and in several cases first-ever book) editions of a large share of Vance's back catalog, plus several reference works about him. The partnership dissolved in 1994; its last book, fittingly, was a second reprint of *The Dying Earth*. Underwood then founded Underwood Books; Miller continued in bookselling until his death in 2015.

Sources used throughout, tagged inline per entry: **[VM]** = vancemuseum.com's *Jack Vance Underwood-Miller Bibliography* (Mike Berro, dated 1996, covers editions "up through 1992," gives limitation/binding detail and month-level dates for the U-M edition specifically); **[ISFDB]** = isfdb.org publisher/title/publication records (crowd-verified against Locus1 and OCLC/WorldCat — primary documentary tier); **[WP]** = the Wikipedia "Underwood–Miller" article's "Books published" list (year-level only, comprehensive but not always precisely dated); **[POP]** = pagesofpages.com's Vance checklist (fan-scholarly, used here only for collection contents I have not independently re-verified against ISFDB — flagged where that's the case); **[VIE.md]** = this project's own `VIE.md`, for cross-checking a title's *true* first publication against its Underwood-Miller edition, which is often a later reprint/reissue rather than the first-ever appearance.

**Important distinction:** many entries below are Underwood-Miller's first *hardcover* edition of a book already available elsewhere in paperback (e.g. the 1981–83 Demon Princes and Durdane reissues), not the story's first-ever publication. Where that applies, it's noted. Where Underwood-Miller *did* publish a genuine book-original or first-ever-in-book-form text, that's also flagged.

## A related but distinct imprint: Brandywyne Books

Underwood-Miller launched **Brandywyne Books** (also spelled "Brandywine" in some sources) in the mid-1980s, in association with Waldenbooks, as a separate limited-editions imprint covering genres beyond SF/fantasy — not simply another Underwood-Miller book line. **[WP]**

- **1984** — *Rhialto the Marvellous* (Dying Earth #4), published under the Brandywyne Books imprint rather than "Underwood-Miller" proper. Contents: Morreion (revised from the 1979 U-M chapbook above); Fader's Waft; The Murthe. **[VIE.md][POP]**
