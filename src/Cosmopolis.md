# Cosmopolis: Issue List
*Cosmopolis* was the newsletter of the Vance Integral Edition (VIE), published monthly (with occasional combined issues) from January 2000 to July 2005, for 63 issues, after which it was succeeded by *Extant* (see `VIE.md` for the VIE's own history). The table below gives, for every issue, its publication date and its lead ("page 1") article or feature — a useful index for locating when a given VIE event, announcement, or essay first appeared.
## Methodology and sourcing
The title for each issue is the heading of the article printed first in that issue (i.e. page 1 of its own table of contents), read directly from the issue's own masthead and contents page — not a secondary paraphrase. Two recurring generic column titles are common early on: "Project Report" and, from #24 onward, "38's Crucible" (Paul Rhoads' monthly editorial), and "Work Tsar Status Report" (Joel Riedesel's production-progress column, later shortened to "Work Tsar Report"). Where the lead item is one of these recurring columns, a short parenthetical indicates that issue's specific topic, drawn from the same issue's table of contents.
Dates are as printed on each issue's own masthead; these agree with the publication dates given on the Foreverness/integralarchive.org download index.
Issue #46 (January 2004) was missing from the local archive as of this writing and has been downloaded from integralarchive.org (`cosmo/Cosmopolis-46.pdf`) to complete the run; all 63 issues are now present locally.
