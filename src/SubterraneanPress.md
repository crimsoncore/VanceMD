# Subterranean Press: A Jack Vance Publication Chronology

Subterranean Press (Burton, Michigan, founded 1995 by William Schafer) became the primary specialty publisher of Jack Vance material in the mid-to-late 2000s, largely coinciding with the Vance Integral Edition's completion and Vance's final years. Most of the fiction volumes below were edited by Terry Dowling and Jonathan Strahan and use VIE-restored texts rather than the original (often magazine-cut) printings.

Sources tagged inline: **[SP]** = subterraneanpress.com's own product pages (publisher-primary, but note the site's "year" metadata field sometimes reflects a later restock/printing date rather than the true first-edition year — flagged where that happened); **[ISFDB]** = isfdb.org publication/title records; **[TD]** = terrydowling.com, Terry Dowling's own bibliography page (as co-editor of every fiction volume below, a primary source for exact dates); **[BG]** = blackgate.com blog coverage (fan-journalism, used only for supplementary detail like individual story dates); **[PW]** = Publishers Weekly reviews.
