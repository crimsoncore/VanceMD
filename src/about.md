# About

This project is a work in progress, and is intended to serve as a resource for fans of Jack Vance's work. The goal is to provide a comprehensive collection of information, links, and resources related to Jack Vance, his writings, and the community that surrounds his work.

Special thanks to the following individuals and organizations for their contributions and support:

- John Vance (Jack Vance's son) for his support and encouragement in preserving his father's legacy.
- Koen Vijverman (spatterlightpress.com) for his work in publishing and preserving Jack Vance's works.
- Wil Ceron - for verifying and providing information on Jack Vance's works and related materials on this site
