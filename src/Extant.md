# Extant: Issue List
*Extant* succeeded *Cosmopolis* as the VIE's newsletter after the main project's 2006 close, continuing under Paul Rhoads' editorship with VIE project updates, essays, and (increasingly, as the project wound down) general Vance-related and off-topic material. It ran for 23 issues, March 2005–January 2008 (see `VIE.md` and the `vance-research` skill's `references/vie-project-history.md` for the surrounding project history).
## Methodology and sourcing
As with `Cosmopolis.md`, the title given for each issue is the heading of its own lead ("page 1") item, read from the issue's own masthead and contents page.
Dates and issue numbers are as printed on each issue's own masthead; these agree with the publication dates given on the Foreverness/integralarchive.org download index.
All 23 issues, plus an alternate "uncensored" printing of #12, were downloaded from integralarchive.org (`cosmo/Extant-NN.pdf`) into `Extant/` and converted to plain text (`pdftotext -layout`) into `Extant/text/`.
**Issue #12 has two versions**: the [standard printing](./extant/Extant-12.pdf) and a separate [uncensored printing](./extant/Extant-12-uncensored.pdf), both retrieved; the difference between the two has not yet been examined.
**Issue #11**: the copy served at `integralarchive.org/cosmo/Extant-11.pdf` is internally malformed at the source (confirmed identical, and equally corrupt, on both the live site and a January 2025 Wayback Machine snapshot; `qpdf`'s structural repair failed too — its page tree is missing entirely, not just its xref table). A working copy was found at the mirror `vanderveeke.net/foreverness/cosmo/Extant-11.pdf` and used instead.

## Notes
- *Extant* was preceded by *Cosmopolis* (63 issues, January 2000–July 2005); see `Cosmopolis.md`.
- Issue #23 is confirmed as the last issue: it documents the VIE corporation's Certificate of Dissolution and was explicitly framed as a final "reprieve" after #22 had already been intended as the close.
